Before we begin

Your questions,
answered.

Can you build for both iOS and Android?

Yes. We scope a shared cross-platform app around your core user journey. Features, backend integrations and store requirements are agreed before development.

Will my website work on phones and tablets?

Responsive layouts adapt the navigation, type and content to smaller screens. The launch process includes checks at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

What do you need to start?

Your goals, service or product details, logo, content and any design references. We then agree on deliverables and a quote.

Are development prices fixed?

The listed development prices are starting points. Your final quote depends on the agreed features, content and integrations. Hosting, domains and app store fees are quoted separately.
